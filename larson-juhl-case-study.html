Larson-Juhl Mobile Commerce Platform

Technical Business Analyst and Delivery Lead for native Android and iOS mobile application development supporting digital commerce workflows, inventory visibility, customer ordering and operational process modernization.

Actively involved across the complete SDLC lifecycle including requirement gathering, stakeholder workshops, sprint planning, backlog grooming, Agile ceremonies, API discussions, UAT coordination, testing reviews, weekly demos, release management and production deployment coordination for both Android (Kotlin) and iOS (Swift) applications.

Business Objective

Larson-Juhl required a scalable mobile commerce ecosystem that would allow customers and internal stakeholders to manage product discovery, inventory checks, ordering workflows and shipment visibility directly from mobile applications while improving operational efficiency and customer engagement.

The objective was to create a seamless native mobile experience across Android and iOS platforms integrated with backend inventory systems, ordering workflows and customer account management processes.
