Online Bidding Platform

Technical Project Manager / Delivery Lead for a real-time auction platform enabling verified users to participate in live-streamed auctions, place bids and complete secure transactions.

Business Problem

Traditional auction systems lacked real-time engagement, automated bidding workflows and centralized management across customers, auctioneers and administrators.

Scope

  • Customer onboarding with KYC verification
  • Live auction participation
  • Real-time bid engine
  • Auto-bid functionality
  • Payment gateway integration
  • Invoices and shipping workflows
  • Auctioneer control panel
  • Admin reporting dashboard

Responsibilities

  • Sprint planning and Agile execution
  • Backend API coordination
  • Cross-team delivery management
  • Risk and dependency tracking
  • UAT planning and production readiness
