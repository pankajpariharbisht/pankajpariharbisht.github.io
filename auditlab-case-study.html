Case Management System

Enterprise Workflow Automation & Document Intelligence Platform

Project Overview

Worked on an enterprise-grade case management and workflow automation platform designed to streamline high-volume document processing, intelligent classification workflows, operational tracking and centralized case lifecycle management.

The platform focused on backend workflow orchestration, scalable document processing pipelines, operational visibility and audit-friendly workflow tracking across enterprise operational environments.

Business Objective

The objective was to improve operational efficiency by enabling intelligent document handling, scalable workflow orchestration, centralized case visibility and streamlined operational tracking across multiple interconnected systems.

The solution aimed to reduce manual operational dependency while improving process standardization, scalability and auditability.

System Architecture

The architecture involved scalable document ingestion workflows, OCR-assisted processing pipelines, AI-assisted classification mechanisms, PostgreSQL synchronization layers, workflow orchestration services and modular case management components.
