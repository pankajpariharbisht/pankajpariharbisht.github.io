Enterprise Document Intelligence & Case Workflow Management Platform

Project Overview

Worked on an enterprise-grade document processing and case management platform designed to automate high-volume PDF ingestion, intelligent document classification, workflow orchestration, and legal case lifecycle management.

The platform focused on backend document intelligence, scalable processing pipelines, audit-friendly metadata generation, and operational workflow optimization across legal and claims-processing environments.

Business Objective

The objective was to streamline operational and legal workflows by enabling intelligent document splitting, AI-assisted classification, scalable data synchronization, and centralized case lifecycle visibility while ensuring operational scalability, explainability, and auditability.

System Architecture

The solution architecture involved AWS-based document ingestion workflows, OCR-assisted PDF processing, AI-powered classification pipelines, PostgreSQL synchronization layers, scalable middleware orchestration, and modular case management components.

Challenges

Key challenges included handling large-scale PDF ingestion, optimizing AI utilization costs, managing legacy-to-modern database synchronization, ensuring explainable document classification outputs, maintaining transaction consistency, and designing scalable operational workflows for high-volume legal and claims-processing environments.

Solution Approach

The overall solution approach focused on combining rule-based workflows with selective AI utilization to balance scalability, explainability, and cost efficiency.

Modular workflows, controlled batch processing, metadata-driven tracking, and scalable synchronization mechanisms were planned to support operational transparency and downstream automation capabilities.

Contributed to designing a scalable operational platform capable of supporting intelligent document processing, automated classification workflows, synchronized case management, and audit-friendly operational visibility.
